Research Summary

Abstract

This research investigates which historical search and engagement signals can help identify content pages showing measurable future decline and therefore deserving earlier human review.

Using March 2026 FlyRank internship warehouse data, the workflow separates historical features from future outcomes, applies client-grouped validation, audits potential leakage, trains an interpretable Decision Tree classifier, and converts predicted decline-risk probabilities into a ranked content action queue.

The final model achieved 72.71% accuracy, 57.98% precision, 12.71% recall, and an F1 score of 20.85% on held-out clients. The system is positioned as directional decision-support rather than proof that individual pages require refreshing.

01 — Research Question

Introduction & Problem Statement

Content teams may manage thousands of pages but have limited time for manual review. A useful machine-learning system should therefore help answer a prioritization question rather than attempt to replace editorial judgment.

Research Question

Which content pages show the strongest measurable signals of refresh opportunity, and how can those pages be ranked for human review using historical search and engagement data?

06 — Honest Framing

Limitations

Proxy Target
Future decline is a measurable proxy, not a ground-truth label that content requires refreshing.
Short Time Window
The study covers March 2026 only.
Observational Study
Association does not establish that refreshing a page causes improved performance.
Low Recall
Recall is 12.71%, meaning many observed decline cases remain undetected.
Analytics Coverage
Average GA4 coverage is only approximately 10.16%.
Generalization
Performance may change across new clients, topics, search conditions, and future periods.

Practical Takeaway

The strongest value of this system is not automatic content editing. It is prioritization.

Historical visibility, CTR, and search position provide measurable signals that can help content teams decide where limited review time should be spent first.

The model therefore acts as a human-in-the-loop decision-support system: machine learning ranks the opportunities, while people investigate the context and choose the final action.
